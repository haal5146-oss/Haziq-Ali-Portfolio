Discover Stage

Connecting college students with niche artists performing in their town, because great music should not go unnoticed.

The goal

What problem were we solving?

Niche and emerging artists performing in college towns rarely get discovered. They play small venues, build real talent, and never reach the students who would love their music, because there is no bridge between them.

Discover Stage set out to be that bridge: a mobile app where local artists post events and fans discover live music right in their college town.

My contribution

What did I build?

  • Originated the core app concept by identifying the gap in artist and fan discovery at the college level
  • Built the backend: a FastAPI service and SQLite database that handle account registration, sign-in with token authentication, password hashing, and artist and fan profiles
  • Ran structured user testing with 15 real users across multiple feedback sessions
  • Analyzed results and translated insights into feature priorities and product decisions
  • Applied market validation before committing to any development scope

User research · Product strategy · React Native · FastAPI · SQLite · Market validation · Feedback analysis · Team collaboration · Agile iteration
