Senior Capstone · 2025–2026

Discover
Stage

Connecting college students with niche artists performing in their town — because great music shouldn't go unnoticed.

The Goal

What problem were we solving?

Niche and emerging artists performing in college towns rarely get discovered. They play small venues, build real talent, and never reach the students who would love their music — because there's no bridge between them. Discover Stage set out to be that bridge: a mobile app where local artists post events and fans discover live music right in their college town.

My Contribution

What did I build?

  • Originated the core app concept — identified the gap in artist-fan discovery at the college level
  • Conducted structured user testing with 15 real users across multiple feedback sessions
  • Analyzed results and translated insights into feature priorities and product decisions
  • Contributed directly to the codebase alongside 3 peers — front-end components and app logic
  • Applied market validation thinking before committing to any development scope
