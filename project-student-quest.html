Software Engineering · 2024–2025

Student
Quest

A unified student hub for social connection, campus services, and academic needs — built from real student voices at CU Boulder.

The Goal

What problem were we solving?

College students juggle academic deadlines, campus events, social plans, and services — but no single platform brings it together. Each need lives in a different app or a flyer on a bulletin board. Student Quest set out to fix that: one centralized hub built around three pillars — social, service, and academic — in one clean, intuitive web platform.

My Contribution

What did I build?

  • Originated the product concept and defined the three-pillar vision: social, service, academic
  • Conducted structured user research with 20 CU Boulder students to validate the concept
  • Oversaw development as product lead for a team of 5 engineers
  • Owned and built the entire front end — HTML, CSS, and JavaScript from scratch
  • Coordinated with a backend teammate to align API structure with UI requirements
  • Translated raw feedback into actionable feature decisions and sprint priorities
